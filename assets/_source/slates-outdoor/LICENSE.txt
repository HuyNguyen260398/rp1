Chosen over Buch's "Outdoor 32x32 tileset" (CC0), which the Phase 3c plan
named: that pack turned out to be a cave/cliff set whose only grass and
water tiles are flat single-colour fills, and it contains no tree at all.
Buch's other outdoor sets are 16x16 or CC-BY-SA. Slates is genuinely
32x32 and carries textured grass, water with a shoreline, and several
trees, so grass, water and oak_tree all come from one coherent pack.
