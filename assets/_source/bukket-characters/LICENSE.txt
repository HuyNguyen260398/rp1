Three 256x128 templates (child, male, woman), each an 8x2 grid of 32x64
frames: four-frame walk cycles for up, down and right (mirror for left).

The BMPs are 24-bit and carry NO alpha channel; the background is a white
chroma key. tools/import_manifest.json declares that colour per slice so
tools/quantize.gd can cut it out.
