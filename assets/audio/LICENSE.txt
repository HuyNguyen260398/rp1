Audio assets. Used as authored -- unlike assets/tiles/, nothing here is
derived. These files are byte-identical to the packs as published.

Source:  Fantozzi's Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone)
         (Fantozzi, via qubodup, CC0 1.0)
         https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
Files:   step_grass_1-3.ogg  <- Fantozzi-Sand{L1,R1,L2}.ogg
         step_dirt_1-3.ogg   <- Fantozzi-Stone{L1,R1,L2}.ogg
Licence: CC0 1.0. No attribution required. Credited in assets/CREDITS.md
         anyway, because the policy table there is a record of what was
         checked, not only of what is owed.

         The surface mapping is the pack author's own: the submission
         reads "'Sand' sounds like grass too. 'Stone' could be most hard
         surfaces". Stone is the dirt path -- a packed path is a hard
         surface, and the alternative packs carry one gravel take, not
         the three the content files need.

Source:  Ambient Bird Sounds (isaiah658, CC0 1.0)
         https://opengameart.org/content/ambient-bird-sounds
Files:   bed_meadow.ogg  <- birds-isaiah658.ogg
Licence: CC0 1.0. The pack's notice reads "Credit to isaiah658 is not
         needed but is appreciated." Appreciated is not required, so this
         is a courtesy credit, not a licence condition.

Every file is Ogg Vorbis. This is load-bearing, not a preference:
AudioStage.set_bed only sets `loop` when the stream is an
AudioStreamOggVorbis, so an mp3 bed would play once and stop.
